Consumer Data Right Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

Current status: Saia da Teoria is preparing its bank connectivity for production. Live Open Banking access will only be enabled after GPF IT SOLUTIONS PTY LTD has been accepted under an appropriate CDR access model. This policy describes the intended handling of CDR data once that access is enabled.

1. Responsible entity

Saia da Teoria is operated by GPF IT SOLUTIONS PTY LTD (ABN 57 638 578 140). Our proposed bank connectivity uses Basiq infrastructure and Basiq’s hosted consent experience. Final CDR roles, accreditation disclosures and supporting-party wording will be updated to match the access model approved before launch.

2. Data we intend to request

With a user’s express consent, the platform intends to request only the account information needed for the bank-feed feature: account name, type and details, balances, and transaction details. We do not intend to request identity, contact, payee or other banking data that is not necessary for this purpose.

3. Purpose

CDR data will be used to import balances and posted transactions into the user’s private Saia da Teoria workspace, reduce manual data entry and provide factual budgeting and personal finance summaries. It will not be used to provide financial product advice, advertising, data brokerage, eligibility decisions or unrelated profiling.

4. Consent and control

A bank connection will begin only after the user completes the hosted consent flow. The consent screen will explain the data requested, purpose and duration. Users will be able to view and manage connections and withdraw consent. We never request or store a user’s online-banking password.

5. Disclosure

We do not sell CDR data. We disclose it only where required to operate the requested service, to approved service providers acting on our instructions, or where required by law. The final production disclosure chain will reflect the CDR access model approved by Basiq/Cuscal.

6. Security and governance

CDR-related credentials are stored server-side. Connections use encrypted transport, incoming webhook signatures are verified, access is tenant-scoped and security-relevant events are logged. Access is limited to authorised personnel and systems with a legitimate operational need.

7. Retention, withdrawal and deletion

We will stop collecting new CDR data when consent expires or is withdrawn. CDR data will be deleted or de-identified when required by the CDR Rules and the approved access model, unless a lawful exception applies. Product records derived from user-entered information are handled under our general Privacy Policy.

8. Complaints

Send CDR questions or complaints to contact@saiadateoria.com. Include enough detail for us to investigate, but do not email banking credentials. We aim to acknowledge complaints promptly and respond within 30 days. Once our access model is approved, this section will also identify the applicable principal or sponsor escalation channel.

9. Policy updates

We will update this policy before live Open Banking is enabled and whenever our CDR role, data practices or regulatory obligations materially change.
