Consumer Data Right policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Current status: Saia da Teoria does not currently collect CDR data through live bank connections. GPF IT SOLUTIONS PTY LTD is not an accredited data recipient and is not yet an active CDR representative. Our CDR representative arrangement with Wych is not yet in force. Bank connectivity will remain unavailable to general users until the arrangement is approved and appears in the official CDR provider register.

1. Who we are

Saia da Teoria is operated by GPF IT SOLUTIONS PTY LTD (ABN 57 638 578 140). We intend to act as a CDR representative of Activate Wych Pty Ltd (“Wych”), a data recipient accredited by the ACCC, which would be our CDR principal. That arrangement is not yet in force. We will publish the accreditation number of our CDR principal here before any live CDR data is collected.

2. Scope

Saia da Teoria is a personal finance and financial-independence tool. With your consent, we would use the Consumer Data Right (CDR) in read-only mode to import your accounts and transactions into your private workspace. We serve individuals only.

3. CDR data we collect

Only what is needed: account information, balances and transactions. We never ask for or store your banking password: you sign in at your bank in the consent flow. Identity, contact and payee data are not requested unless a clearly disclosed feature needs them and the arrangement allows it.

4. Purposes

Budgeting, net-worth tracking and categorising your transactions. We do not sell this data or use it for advertising, data brokerage, credit or eligibility decisions, financial product advice or unrelated profiling.

5. Disclosure and service providers

We do not disclose CDR data to other accredited data recipients or to non-accredited persons, and we do not use it for direct marketing. Data is held by infrastructure providers (Supabase, Railway and Vercel), which may process it in Australia or other countries; they are also listed in our Privacy Policy. We may disclose data where the law requires. We would receive the data through Wych, our CDR principal.

6. Consent and withdrawal

You consent in the consent flow provided by Wych and your bank. Consent lasts up to 365 days and the expiry date is shown in the app. You can withdraw it at any time: in Saia da Teoria, with Wych or with your bank. When you withdraw, we stop collecting data; without the connection you lose automatic import but can still enter transactions manually.

7. Notifications about your consent

The app shows the connection status and the consent expiry date. If a data breach involves your CDR data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Access and correction

You can ask for access to or correction of your data at contact@saiadateoria.com. We reply in writing within 30 days, saying what was done or why it could not be. You can also view and adjust your records in the app.

9. Retention and deletion

When you disconnect a bank, its imported data is deleted by default; you can choose to keep it as history. If consent expires or is withdrawn outside the app, we delete the data at the next daily check, within one day. Manual entries are not affected. You can also delete your account in Profile. Other personal information is covered by our Privacy Policy.

10. Complaints

Email contact@saiadateoria.com with your details and what happened, and do not include banking credentials. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact Wych, the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA) or the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).

11. How to verify our status

Before connecting an account, check Saia da Teoria and Wych in the Australian Government’s current provider register. If the arrangement is not visible there, live CDR connectivity will not be offered.

12. Availability and updates

This policy is on our website. For a copy, email contact@saiadateoria.com. We will update it before live CDR access is enabled to reflect the registered arrangement and our actual practices.
