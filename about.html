About

Clarity before complexity.

Saia da Teoria is a private personal finance workspace built to help people organise the facts of their financial life and review them with less friction.

Why it exists

Financial information is often scattered.

Accounts, debts, investments, cash flow and goals often live in different apps and spreadsheets. Saia da Teoria brings those records into one structured workspace so invited users can see their position more clearly.

The product provides organisational tools and factual summaries. It does not recommend financial products or replace advice from an appropriately licensed professional.
