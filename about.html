About

Less theory. More clarity about your money.

Saia da Teoria is Portuguese for “get out of theory”. It is a private workspace for people who want to see where they actually stand: what they own, what they owe and where each month goes.

Where it started

It began as a spreadsheet.

The first version was a personal finance workbook used to mentor people through their money: assets and debts, the monthly flow, and how far they were from a target.

Spreadsheets get fragile the moment more than one person depends on them. So the same structure became an application, with the numbers kept in one place and the history kept intact.

What it is not

No advice, no sales.

Saia da Teoria organises the information you give it and shows factual summaries. It does not recommend investments or credit products, and it does not move money.

Automatic bank connections are not available to general users yet. The current status is explained in the CDR readiness statement. We never ask for your online-banking password.
